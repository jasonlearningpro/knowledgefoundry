Check the course for problems

The Validation panel lists everything the checks find, as you work. Open it from the right-hand rail, or choose Publish → Check the course for problems.

  • Errors (codes starting VAL-E) stop the course from being exported: a missing wire from Start, a variable that does not exist, a formula that cannot be read, fast flashing over a large area, and so on.
  • Warnings (VAL-W) do not stop an export, but say what a learner may run into: a missing alt text or caption, a button with no wire, text that is hard to read on its background, a reading order that runs against how a screen looks.
  • Select a finding to go to the card, and the item, it is about. With the keyboard, Tab to a finding and press Enter.
  • Text laid over a shape or box (a pop-up's words on its white box, for example) is checked against the color of that shape, not only the page background. If the words are hard to read on it, the warning names the text and the shape: change the text color or the shape's fill.
  • The Export dialog shows the same checks for the format you chose (see Publish the course (export)).
