Choose light or dark (Appearance)

The editor can look light, dark or like your computer. This changes only the editor you work in. The course on the stage, the live preview and the finished course keep the colors you gave them.

  1. Open Appearance in the bottom right corner of the window and choose Light, Dark, System (follow Windows) or one of four color schemes (below). You can also use View > Appearance, or press Ctrl+K and type "appearance".
  2. System is the choice you start with. It uses the Windows setting, and the editor changes by itself when Windows changes between light and dark.
  3. The choice is remembered on this computer and is not saved in your courses, so a course looks the same when someone else opens it.

Color schemes. Four more choices change the editor's colors, and the Flow Canvas with them:

  • Foundry Warm Slate (dark): charcoal with warm copper and amber accents, off-white lines between cards, and orange where a path is running. Easy on the eyes for long sessions.
  • Blueprint Navy (dark): deep navy with a faint grid, and electric cyan and white lines, like an engineering drawing. Wires stay sharp when you zoom out over a big course.
  • Studio Parchment (light): a warm, low-glare linen background with espresso text, soft shadows under the cards, and terracotta, olive and indigo lines.
  • Synth-Chroma (dark): a near-black canvas where each kind of card has its own color: violet for decisions, emerald for questions, quizzes and results, cyan for screens and amber for variables. Lines and ports take the color of the card they leave, so you can follow a path by color.

Every scheme keeps text easy to read, and controls and focus outlines easy to see. The course itself is never recolored.
