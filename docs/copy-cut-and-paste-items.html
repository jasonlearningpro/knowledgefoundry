Copy, cut and paste items

Select one or more items on the stage, then press Ctrl+C (or Edit > Copy) to copy them, or Ctrl+X to cut them. Press Ctrl+V to paste. You can paste on the same screen or select another screen first and paste there. Each paste lands a little down and to the right of the last one so they do not sit exactly on top of each other. A pasted button is a new button with its own socket on the Flow Canvas, not wired to anything. Items that were grouped are pasted as a new group. Ctrl+D (Duplicate) still makes a copy right away on the same screen. When you are typing in a text box, Copy and Paste work on the text as usual.

Shapes you have not named are listed by their kind in the timeline and the Layers panel: Rectangle, Ellipse, Star and so on. If there is more than one of a kind they are numbered, Rectangle 1, Rectangle 2, in the order you added them. Double-click a name in the Layers panel to give a shape a name of your own.
