Motion, flashing and sound

Check course looks at how things on each screen really move over time, and tells you when something could be a problem for learners:

FindingWhat it meansHow to fix it
Error: an item flashes more than 3 times a second and covers a large part of the screenFast flashing over a large area can cause seizures. You cannot export until it is fixed.Slow it down (fewer fade in and out changes, or longer effects), or make the item smaller.
Warning: a smaller item flashes more than 3 times a secondIt is hard to read around and distracting.Keep it to 3 flashes a second or fewer.
Warning: movement starts by itself and lasts over 5 seconds, and the Play / pause button is offLearners cannot stop it.Turn Play / pause on in the Player designer, or shorten the movement.
Warning: sound starts by itself and lasts over 3 seconds, and Play / pause and Volume are both offLearners cannot stop it or turn it down.Turn one of those buttons on in the Player designer.

With the player's normal settings you will not see the last two. Learners who have asked their computer for less motion see every item at rest, with no effects. A course that has sound starts from a Start button that the learner presses (a course without sound starts straight away), and a screen never moves on by itself. The check works from what the item really does, so it also covers effects added by plugins.
