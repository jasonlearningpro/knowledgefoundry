Select, group and arrange items

Select several items: hold Shift or Ctrl and click items on the stage to add or remove them, or drag a rectangle on an empty part of the stage to select everything it touches (hold Shift or Ctrl to add to what is selected). Ctrl+A selects everything on the screen. In the Layers panel, Shift or Ctrl-click does the same. Esc, or a click on empty stage, clears the selection.

Move them together: drag any selected item, or use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps). The selection snaps to the edges and center of the stage and the other items; hold Alt to turn snapping off.

Align and space: when several items are selected a small palette appears with Align buttons (left, center, right, top, middle, bottom) and Space evenly (left to right, top to bottom; it needs three or more items). Align lines the items up with the edge of the whole selection; with one item selected, Align lines it up with the stage. The same actions are in the command search (Ctrl+K, type "align" or "space"), so you can do them without a mouse.

Stretch and turn them together: with several items selected, the dashed box around them has handles. Drag a handle to stretch all of them with the box (Shift on a corner keeps the proportions). Drag the round handle above it to turn them all together (Shift snaps to 15 degrees). Edges snap to the stage and to other items while you stretch; hold Alt to switch snapping off.

Equal gaps: while you drag an item, it snaps so the gap beside it matches a gap that is already there, or so it sits midway between two items. Pink marks show the equal gaps.

Select from the keyboard: click the stage, then press Alt+Right (or Alt+Down) for the next item and Alt+Left (or Alt+Up) for the previous one. Add Shift to add the item to the selection instead.

Group: press Group (Ctrl+G) to tie items together. Clicking any item in a group selects the whole group, and the group moves, copies, deletes, stretches and turns together (it gets the same box and handles as any selection). Ungroup (Ctrl+Shift+G) separates them. To change just one item in a group, select it in the Layers panel. A group does not change how its items animate or when they appear: each keeps its own timing and effects. A ⛓ in the Layers panel marks grouped items.

Copy and delete: Ctrl+D copies the whole selection (the copies are selected, and a copied group becomes a new group), and Del deletes it. Each action is one Undo.
