Set the course look once (the master theme)

Click Master theme… on the toolbar.

  1. Pick a ready-made master, or set your own: the six colors (Primary is used for buttons), background (color, gradient or a picture from your imported images), fonts (body and headings), and text sizes.
  2. Every screen follows it, including template screens and screens you add later. Change a master setting and all the screens update.
  3. To make one screen different, select it and tick Override master theme in the Properties tab. The color, font and background settings appear only after you tick it, and only the ones you change are overridden; click the ↺ beside a setting to go back to the master. Untick the box to return the whole screen to the master.
  4. On the stage, the Color / Fill / Text buttons show the theme colors first. Pick a theme color and it will follow the master if you change the theme colors later. Auto means "use the master".
  5. If text is hard to read on the background, the dialog and the Validation tab warn you.
